# Write in my voice

**Use when:** you write anything that goes out under the broker's name: an email, a reply, a text,
a message with a meeting invite. Also the first time, to learn their voice.

## My voice

Not learned yet. Until it is, write short, plain and friendly, and tell the broker you haven't
learned their voice yet.

- **Greeting:** not learned yet
- **Sign-off:** not learned yet
- **Length:** not learned yet
- **Tone:** not learned yet
- **Language:** not learned yet
- **Words and phrases I use:** not learned yet
- **Things I never write:** em dashes (they make writing look AI-made). The rest not learned yet

## Learning the voice

Ask the broker first, for example: "Want me to learn how you write from your sent emails? I'll read
20 to 30 of them and keep only your style, no client details."

1. Read 20 to 30 recent emails the broker wrote themselves. Skip forwards, automatic mails and
   mails someone else wrote for them.
2. Fill in "My voice" above: how they greet, sign off and close, how long they write, their tone
   with clients and with partners, the language they use with whom, the words they use and avoid.
3. Add two or three short examples, with every client detail taken out (hard rule 1 in
   `AGENTS.md`).
4. Show the broker the result in a few lines, and fix what they say is off.
5. Offer a Grok skill named "Write in my voice", described as "Use whenever you write an email or a
   message as the broker", with the text "Follow skills/write-in-my-voice.md in
   /workspace/ai-brain". It makes every chat use the voice by itself ([[skills/README]]).

## Writing

1. Follow "My voice": greeting, sign-off, length, words.
2. Put the point in the first line, and end on one clear next step.
3. When the broker changes something you wrote, notice what they changed. A pattern worth keeping
   goes into "My voice", after you check it with them.
