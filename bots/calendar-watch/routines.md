# Calendar Watch: routines

A routine is a job this bot does by itself on a schedule. Routines run inside Grok. This file is
the record of them, so the broker and every bot can see what runs, and a routine can be rebuilt if
this bot is ever made again. How to set one up: [[skills/set-up-a-routine]].

## Routines to offer

Suggestions, never defaults. Offer the ones that fit what you saw in the broker's calendar, with
times usually inside their working hours. The weekly look-ahead belongs to the Daily Brief.

- **Tomorrow check:** clashes, missing links and unanswered invites for the next day. Often once on
  weekday evenings.
- **Meeting prep:** prep notes for the day's client meetings. Often once each working morning.
- **Meeting summaries:** a summary after each meeting that has notes or a recording, in the brain
  and, if the broker wants it, in their own document ([[skills/meeting-summary]]). Often once at
  the end of each working day.
