# How I work

How the broker likes to work with all of their bots: how they want to be told things, when they
don't want to be disturbed, what they find helpful and what annoys them, who on their team handles
what (such as "Sam, my assistant, answers lender emails"), and the suggestions they said no to. One
dated line each, newest at the bottom. A preference for one bot only goes in that bot's `job.md`. A
firm always or never goes in `rules/my-rules.md`.
