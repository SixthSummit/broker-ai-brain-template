# My rules

The broker's own always-and-never rules for every bot, such as "Never email clients after 6 pm" or
"Always copy my assistant on lender emails". Every bot follows them. When the broker says
"always" or "never" about how their bots should work, read the wording back to them, then add it
here as a dated line. A rule for one bot only goes in that bot's `job.md` instead. If a rule names a
partner, such as the broker's assistant, make sure they are in `context/my-people.md`.

If one of these clashes with `rules/ground-rules.md`, the ground rule wins: tell the broker. If they
want the ground rule itself changed, "Hard rules" in `AGENTS.md` says how, and which ones never
change.
